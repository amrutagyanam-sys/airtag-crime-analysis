#  Tracking or Stalking?
## A Cybersecurity Research Brief on Apple AirTag Misuse 
**Author:** Amrutha Gyanam  
**Research Focus:** Privacy • Data Governance • Digital Safety • Responsible Technology  

This repository presents an academic cybersecurity research project examining the misuse of Apple AirTags for **cyberstalking, unauthorized tracking, coercive control, vehicle tracking**, and **IoT-enabled surveillance**.

Originally developed as graduate research, the project examines the intersection of technology, **privacy, human safety, governance, and responsible technology use**. It combines technical analysis with real-world misuse scenarios to identify privacy and safety risks and consider potential governance and mitigation approaches.

# Key Topics
Apple AirTag misuse in cyberstalking and unauthorized tracking
Bluetooth Low Energy and Find My network privacy considerations
Anti-stalking alert limitations across iOS and Android users
IoT-enabled surveillance and vehicle-tracking risks
Privacy, consent, safety, and governance gaps
Mitigation considerations for users, technology providers, and policymakers
